Sur- 是字首，代表的是不斷前進與超越。

"澀"一詞則是形容沉著雅致、內斂質樸、寧靜而深刻的東方美學。

我們將當代的思維結合風土、飲食文化、情懷與人，並強調自然與工藝的平衡，創作出獨一無二的台灣料理。

乘著光影看見年月，在新舊交融的空間內，詮釋著我們所生活的台灣。

Sur- is the prefix, representing continuous progress and transcendence.

The term "澀 (Sè)" describes a profound Oriental aesthetic that is reserved, refined, tranquil, and deeply contemplative.

We infuse contemporary thought with local terroir, culinary culture, heartfelt stories, and people — emphasizing the balance between nature and craft to create a uniquely Taiwanese cuisine.

Through shifting light and shadow, we observe the passage of time. Within a space where old and new converge, we interpret the Taiwan in which we live.
